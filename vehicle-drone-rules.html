Vehicles and Drones

Vehicles are tool proficiencies in fifth edition, not skills. In Stellar Dawn, they cover spacecraft, planetary craft, mechs, and other vehicle-scale machines. Drones use their own handling rules unless they are large enough to function like vehicles.

Vehicles

Choose a Category

When a background, feature, training option, or other rule grants proficiency with vehicles, choose one Stellar Dawn vehicle category approved by the DM.

Space

Vehicles (Spacecraft)

Starships, shuttles, fighters, cutters, boarding craft, and other vessels meant for spaceflight.

Ground

Vehicles (Planetary)

Rovers, crawlers, trucks, grav-bikes, skimmers, industrial haulers, and other surface vehicles.

Frames

Vehicles (Mech)

Walkers, powered frames, industrial loaders, combat mechs, and machines piloted through body-scale movement.

Operation

Vehicle Checks

Use vehicle proficiency when the challenge is piloting, maneuvering, navigating, or operating the vehicle rather than repairing, hacking, or understanding it.

Situation Likely Check Use This When
Evasive flying Dexterity (Vehicles: Spacecraft) The pilot is making tight maneuvers, dodging fire, or threading a dangerous approach.
Calculated maneuver Intelligence (Vehicles: Spacecraft) The pilot is using timing, vectors, ship limits, or tactical positioning.
Read traffic or hazards Wisdom (Vehicles: Spacecraft) The pilot is relying on field judgment, pattern recognition, or instinctive hazard sense.
Force a mech through resistance Strength (Vehicles: Mech) The machine amplifies the pilot's body movement and the challenge is physical force.

Drones

Separate the Fiction

Drones should not automatically use Animal Handling. Choose the proficiency based on whether the character is piloting, repairing, hacking, or dealing with a living creature.

Drone Rig

Owned or Authorized Drones

A Drone Rig is used to pilot, coordinate, recover, and operate mechanical drones. Use Dexterity for tight manual piloting, Wisdom for scouting and overwatch, and Intelligence for programming, diagnostics, or coordination.

Intrusion

Hijacked Drones

Use Intelligence (Intrusion Tools) when a character is seizing unauthorized control, spoofing ownership, corrupting a command link, or disabling hostile drone behavior.

Repair

Damaged Drones

Use Intelligence (Engineer's Tools) when a drone needs repair, modification, diagnostics, fabrication, salvage, or mechanical adjustment.

Creatures

Living Drones and Bioforms

Use Animal Handling for creatures with instincts, fear, hunger, training, pain, or trust. A living drone, organic machine, or bonded bio-construct may call for Animal Handling, Nature, or Arcana at the DM's discretion.

Travel

Navigation and Sensors

Travel checks depend on whether the character is moving the vehicle, plotting the route, making a map, or noticing something right now.

Routes

Navigator's Tools

Navigator's Tools cover route plotting, jump calculations, stellar cartography, drift correction, signal triangulation, and travel when ordinary maps are incomplete.

Maps

Cartographer's Tools

Cartographer's Tools cover survey mapping, colony layouts, station plans, stellar cartography, jump-lane notes, planetary scans, and making reliable maps from incomplete information.

Sensors

Perception or Investigation

Use Wisdom (Perception) to notice a contact, hazard, or detail now. Use Intelligence (Investigation) to interpret sensor records, compare patterns, or work out what the data means.

Campaign Nexus. Vehicle and drone proficiencies give pilots, scouts, engineers, and infiltrators different ways to matter.
