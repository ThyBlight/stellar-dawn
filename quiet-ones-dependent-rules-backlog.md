# Quiet Ones Dependent Rules Backlog

These items should wait until The Quiet Ones establishes how magical, metaphysical, or supernatural the larger Stellar Dawn campaign becomes.

- Spell components: verbal, somatic, material, costly components, and what disrupts them.
- Spellcasting foci: arcane, divine, druidic, musical, pact, and class-specific equivalents.
- Radiant and necrotic damage flavor.
- Resurrection, death recovery, revivify-style effects, and whether supernatural return from death exists.
- Class flavor lanes for cleric, paladin, druid, ranger, warlock, monk, bard, and other fantasy-forward classes.
- Summons, familiars, beast companions, spirits, drones, constructs, and conjured creatures.
- Fey, fiends, celestials, undead, elementals, planes, spirits, and other cosmological creature categories.
- Final Arcana framing if the campaign lands closer to hard science, Spelljammer-style magic, anomaly science, or a mix.
