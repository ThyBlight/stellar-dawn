A year before the quiet arrives

The premise

This is a game about a small community living through its last quiet year before something labelled "The Quiet Ones" arrive, occur, or awaken. We will play it as a prelude to our future Dungeons & Dragons 5e campaign, using the community's choices, losses, landmarks, fears, and unfinished work to help shape the world that campaign begins in.

The promise

Together, we will give the future campaign a lived-in past. The places we draw, the choices the community makes, and the questions we leave open will become history for the D&D campaign that follows.
